M6 — does capacity buy memorisation?
==============================================================================

4,000 narratives per set, three seeds per arm, F1 %.

A transfer curve, easiest to hardest. Overfitting is a claim about the
SHAPE of this curve: a memorising model starts high and falls further.

  arm                   seen        stratified           natural         surrogate   drop 1->4
  micro           96.6 ±0.4         94.7 ±0.5         95.2 ±0.3         85.5 ±0.5        11.1
  base2           98.2 ±0.1         92.0 ±0.6         95.1 ±0.2         80.0 ±2.9        18.1
  large           98.2 ±0.1         91.5 ±1.5         95.0 ±0.2         80.1 ±1.9        18.2

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
READING IT

  micro loses 11.1 F1 points across the curve;
  large loses 18.2.  Difference: +7.0 points.

  The larger arm falls FURTHER. That is the overfitting signature:
  it holds up on training phrasings and gives more of it back on
  unseen ones. Hypothesis (b) is supported.

  Note: this tests transfer across CARRIER PHRASING. It does not test
  hypothesis (a) -- whether 15,000 examples underdetermine 434M
  parameters -- which needs a training-set-size sweep, not an eval.
