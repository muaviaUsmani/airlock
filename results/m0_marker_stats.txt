The spread between p5 and p95 here is the inconsistency the brief
warns about. A tight spread means the scrubbing standard was applied
evenly and recall against it is meaningful. A wide spread means some
narratives were scrubbed far harder than others, and a model that
matches the average will look wrong on both tails.

A run of 3+ usually means one entity — a full name, a street address —
was replaced word by word. Runs inflate any per-marker count, so span
level metrics must merge them. That merge rule goes in DEFINITIONS.md.
