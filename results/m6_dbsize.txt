M6 — does re-identification survive a bigger database?
==================================================================

Population is the assumption most likely to be quoted against the
headline. Transactions per customer are held constant, so the only
thing changing is how many people the attacker must tell apart.

   customers method       U unique    K set<5  attacker fp
       2,500 raw             19.1%      20.4%         1.8%
       2,500 presidio         0.2%       0.2%         0.2%
      10,000 raw             36.9%      44.3%         0.5%
      10,000 presidio         1.2%       3.2%         0.4%
      40,000 raw             14.3%      29.9%         1.4%
      40,000 presidio         0.1%       0.2%         0.3%

raw text: U goes 19.1% -> 14.3% as the database grows 2,500 -> 40,000
